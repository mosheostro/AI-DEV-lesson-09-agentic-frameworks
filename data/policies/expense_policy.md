# Expense Policy

Owner: Finance
Effective date: 2026-06-01

## Approval thresholds

Normal expenses under USD 250 need a receipt and manager approval. Expenses over
USD 250 require Finance approval in addition to the manager's — the manager's
approval alone is not sufficient once the threshold is crossed.

## What goes through a different process

- Software purchases follow the SaaS Procurement Policy even when the amount is
  small — expensing a software subscription instead of routing it through
  procurement skips the vendor and security review that policy exists for.
- Remote office accessories (monitors, docks, chairs) usually go through the
  Equipment Policy as a provisioned item, not as a personal reimbursement.

## Submission window

Expenses should be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date. Expenses
submitted later than that need a manager's note explaining the delay before
Finance will process them — this keeps expense records close enough to the
purchase that receipts and business justification are still easy to verify.

## Non-reimbursable items

Personal subscriptions, commuting costs, and items already covered by the
Equipment Policy are not reimbursable through the expense process — trying to
expense them just gets redirected to the correct policy, not approved twice.
