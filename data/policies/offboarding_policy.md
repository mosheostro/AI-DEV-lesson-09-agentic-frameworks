# Offboarding Policy

Owner: HR and IT
Effective date: 2026-06-01

## Ownership split

HR owns employment status (the official last working day and separation type), IT
owns account suspension and device return, and managers own handoff notes (what the
departing employee was working on, and who picks it up). Each party acts on its own
piece without waiting for the others to finish theirs.

## Removal timing

Highly restricted systems — BambooHR, AWS, Okta admin, GitHub admin, and payroll
tools — must be removed on the last working day, or immediately for urgent
separations (e.g., involuntary terminations, security-related departures). "Last
working day" means access is cut at end of that day, not sometime during the
following week.

## Equipment return

The employee's managed laptop, and any additional equipment issued under the
Equipment Policy (monitor, dock, headset), must be returned to IT within 5 business
days of the last working day. For remote employees, IT arranges a prepaid return
shipment rather than leaving equipment return to the employee's own expense.

## Knowledge transfer

The manager's handoff notes should cover in-flight work, any credentials or
accounts that only the departing employee knew about, and open commitments to
customers or vendors. Handoff notes go in the team's shared documentation, not in a
private message that leaves with the employee's account.
