# SaaS Procurement Policy

Owner: Finance
Effective date: 2026-06-01

## Approval threshold

Older threshold: SaaS expansions above USD 5,000 require Finance approval. This is
superseded for Q3 2026 by the SaaS renewal freeze memo, which sets the threshold at
USD 7,500. Where the two disagree, the more recent memo governs.

## Before approving an expansion

Before approving a SaaS expansion (new seats, a new tier, or a new vendor), check:

- Contract term — does the current term even allow expansion mid-cycle?
- Seat usage — are existing seats near capacity, or is there room already?
- Renewal date — is a renewal coming up soon enough to fold this into that review?
- Owner department — who requested it and who is accountable for the spend?
- Security review — has this vendor cleared NovaOps's security requirements for
  the data it will touch?

## Process

Requests start with the owning department describing the business need, then go to
Finance for the threshold check above. Finance may loop in Security or IT
depending on what the vendor touches — that routing decision is Finance's call, not
the requester's.

## Seat-limited software

Some software (Webex is a recurring example) can hit its seat cap even when the
department is technically eligible under this policy. In that case the request is
not a procurement decision anymore — it is a capacity issue that IT tracks
separately until seats are reclaimed or an expansion is approved.
