# Contract Renewal Policy

Owner: Finance and Legal Ops
Effective date: 2026-06-01

## Purpose

This policy governs how NovaOps reviews vendor contracts before they renew, so
renewals are a deliberate decision rather than something that happens automatically
because nobody looked at the contract in time.

## Review timing

Review renewals 90 days before the contract's end date. If the renewal notice
deadline falls within 45 days of today, flag the renewal as urgent so it is not
missed while sitting in a routine review queue.

## What the review covers

Each review checks:

- Seat usage — are we paying for licenses nobody is using?
- Data sensitivity — what NovaOps or customer data does this vendor touch?
- Support performance — has the vendor met its SLA over the term?
- Security review — is the vendor still current on NovaOps's security requirements?
- Owner department — who is accountable for this relationship internally?
- Risky clauses — auto-renewal terms, liability caps, exit penalties, and similar.

## Escalation

Ambiguous renewal terms (unclear pricing, vague auto-renewal language, disputed
scope) go to Finance for a ruling before the renewal proceeds. The owning department
does not have authority to interpret ambiguous contract language on its own.

## Record-keeping

The reviewer's findings and the renewal decision are kept with the contract record,
so the next review cycle can see what changed (or didn't) since the last one.
