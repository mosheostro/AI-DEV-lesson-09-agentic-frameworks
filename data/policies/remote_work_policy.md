# Remote Work Policy

Owner: HR
Effective date: 2026-06-01

## Work model

NovaOps is remote-first with hybrid hubs for teams and roles that benefit from
regular in-person time. Remote employees may request a monitor and dock. Hybrid
employees may request the same bundle, but only with an approved addendum on file
— the addendum is what distinguishes an occasional hybrid visitor from someone
provisioned for a hub desk.

## Working securely from home

Working remotely does not relax NovaOps's security requirements — it just moves
where they apply. Remote employees must use MFA (per the Security and MFA Policy)
on every login, keep their home Wi-Fi on WPA2/WPA3 with a non-default password, and
avoid working on customer or source-code data over public Wi-Fi without the company
VPN. A managed laptop's disk encryption and screen lock are not optional to disable
for convenience.

## Location changes

Location changes must be reported to HR before payroll, benefits, tax, or equipment
decisions are made — a move across state or country lines can change withholding,
benefits eligibility, and even which equipment vendor can ship there, so HR needs
advance notice rather than finding out after the fact.
