# Onboarding Policy

Owner: HR
Effective date: 2026-06-01

## Who does what

HR creates the employee's profile, employment packet, and onboarding checklist. IT
creates Okta, the device record, and baseline access — but only after the offer is
signed, never before. Provisioning ahead of a signed offer is not permitted, even to
save time for a fast-moving start date.

## Role-based baseline access

Baseline access varies by role. Customer Success Managers specifically need Okta,
Slack, Notion, Salesforce, Webex, BambooHR employee self-service, and SupportDesk
viewer — this is the standard bundle for that role, not something each manager
requests individually.

## Checklist discipline

Do not mark onboarding tasks complete unless the system of record (the onboarding
database) says complete. A task that looks done from a Slack message or a verbal
confirmation is not the same as a task marked complete in the checklist — the
checklist is the source of truth that other teams rely on.

## Blocked access stays blocked

If Webex access is blocked by a seat issue, it should remain marked blocked in the
checklist until the seat issue is actually resolved — do not mark it complete as a
courtesy or because the rest of onboarding finished on time. A blocked item stays
visible so it doesn't get silently dropped.
