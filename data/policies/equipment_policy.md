# Equipment Policy

Owner: IT
Effective date: 2026-06-01

## Purpose

This policy defines what hardware NovaOps provides by role and work arrangement,
and the rules for personal equipment.

## Standard issue

- Full-time employees receive a managed laptop.
- Contractors normally use registered BYOD (their own device, enrolled in NovaOps's
  device management so it meets the same baseline security requirements as a
  managed laptop).
- Remote or hybrid employees with an approved addendum may receive a monitor and
  dock, in addition to their laptop.
- Customer Success Managers may receive a headset, given how much of their work is
  calls.

## Personal laptops

Personal laptops are not allowed for employees, with one narrow exception: a
five-business-day IT continuity exception when a managed laptop is being repaired
or replaced and the employee has no working device. IT must approve and log the
exception before the employee uses a personal machine, even for those five days.

## Care and return

Equipment remains NovaOps property. Employees are expected to take reasonable care
of it — a lost charger is a shrug, a laptop left unlocked in a coffee shop is not.
Accidental damage during normal use is covered by IT at no cost to the employee;
loss or damage from clear negligence (e.g., leaving a laptop visibly unattended in
an unlocked car) may be charged back at IT's discretion, case by case.

All equipment must be returned through IT at offboarding or role change, per the
Offboarding Policy — it is not something a departing employee keeps or a manager
signs off on informally.
