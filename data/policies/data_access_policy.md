# Data Access Policy

Owner: Security and HR
Effective date: 2026-06-01

## Purpose and precedence

This policy governs who can see what data at NovaOps. It overrides general access
documents wherever they conflict — if the Access Management Policy is silent or
ambiguous about a specific piece of data, this policy is the tie-breaker.

## Principle: least privilege

Access to data follows least privilege by role and sensitivity: an employee gets
the minimum access their job requires, not the maximum the system allows. This
applies to viewing, exporting, and sharing alike.

## Data sensitivity levels

- **Public** — safe for anyone, inside or outside the company (marketing pages, the
  public help center).
- **Internal** — for NovaOps employees and approved contractors (handbook pages,
  internal wikis).
- **Confidential** — for the specific team or role that owns it (customer contracts,
  security reviews).
- **Restricted** — for named individuals only, with an audit trail (salary data,
  performance reviews, legal matters).

## Salary and self-service data

BambooHR salary fields are HR-only. Regular employees may view only their own
self-service information — their own PTO, pay stubs, and profile, never a
coworker's. A regular employee asking for another employee's salary data must
receive a permission denial and a safe HR alternative (e.g., "I can't share that —
here's how to reach HR Business Partners if you have a compensation question").

## Audit logging

Access to Confidential and Restricted data is logged, including who viewed what and
when. Logs are reviewed by Security on a monthly cadence and on any reported
incident.
