# Acceptable Use Policy

Owner: IT
Effective date: 2026-06-01

## Purpose

NovaOps systems, accounts, and devices are provided for company work. This policy
defines what counts as acceptable use and what employees and contractors must not
do with company systems, regardless of whether they are on a managed device or a
registered BYOD machine.

## Prohibited storage

Do not store source code, customer data, or employment records in personal
accounts of any kind — personal email, personal cloud drives, or personal note-taking
apps. This applies even for short-term or "just this once" convenience, since a
personal account is outside NovaOps's access controls and audit logging.

## External sharing

External sharing of company data must go through approved systems and follow least
privilege — share only what the recipient needs, for only as long as they need it.
Personal cloud-sync tools (Dropbox, personal Google Drive, personal OneDrive, etc.)
are not allowed for company files under any circumstance, including drafts.

## Enforcement

A first violation typically results in a written warning and mandatory retraining;
repeated or deliberate violations (e.g., knowingly exfiltrating customer data) are
handled as a security incident and escalated to IT Security and HR together. When in
doubt about whether a tool or a sharing method is approved, ask IT before using it —
not after.
